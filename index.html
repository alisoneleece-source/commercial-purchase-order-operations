PROTOTYPE — SIMULATED DATA & SERVER CALLS

Interactive prototype of a commercial purchase-order management system I designed and built. The original workflow automated email intake, PO extraction, version tracking, document filing, approval routing, and status updates. All companies, names, identifiers, amounts, and documents shown are fictional.

How it worksFrom inbox to completed order
1

Email + PDF arrive

The commercial inbox is checked automatically for new purchase orders.

2

Registry captures it

A private source-of-truth record stores the email, document and workflow state.

3

Extract + version

PO details are extracted, duplicates identified and revisions assigned V2, V3 and beyond.

4

Review in dashboard

Account, country, currency, SFID, notes and next action are checked in one place.

5

Submit + complete

Approved orders move to Salesforce and the Order Book, then settle at the bottom as Done.
