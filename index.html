Commercial Purchase Order Operations System

Automated intake · validation · version control · document filing · team review
Prototype · simulated data and interactions

Interactive prototype of a commercial purchase-order operations system I designed and built. The original workflow automated email intake, PO extraction, duplicate detection, version tracking, regional routing, document filing, approval decisions and status updates. All companies, names, identifiers, amounts and documents shown are fictional.

How it worksFrom inbox to completed order
1

Email + PDF arrive

The commercial inbox is checked automatically for new purchase orders.

2

Registry captures it

A private source-of-truth record stores the email, document and workflow state.

3

Extract + version

PO details are extracted, duplicates identified and revisions assigned V2, V3 and beyond.

4

Review in dashboard

Account, country, currency, SFID, notes and next action are checked in one place.

5

Approve and hand off

After the SFID is confirmed, approval updates the next action for submission and filing.
